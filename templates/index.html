Everything a matching engine needs

Five focused pages cover the full lifecycle of an order — from submission to execution.

P/T

Price-Time Priority

The best price always trades first. Ties at the same price are resolved strictly FIFO, by arrival order.

½

Partial Fills

Large orders can be filled across several counterparties. Remaining quantity keeps resting in the book.

✓

Input Validation

Order IDs, prices, quantities and sides are all checked server-side before an order ever reaches the book.

⇄

Live Order Book

Buy orders sorted highest price first, sell orders sorted lowest price first — updated after every match.

⟲

Execution History

Every trade the engine produces is logged permanently with buyer, seller, price, quantity and timestamp.

Σ

Analytics Dashboard

Best bid, best ask, spread and last traded price — recalculated live from the in-memory book.
